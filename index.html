Support

Help Center

Guidance for using Rhymo's website and account features.

How do I sign in?

Choose Login with Discord and approve the requested permissions. The backend validates the callback and creates a session.

Why can't I log in?

Check that the backend is deployed and the Discord redirect URL exactly matches the configured callback. Safari privacy protections can block cross-site cookies.

How do I add Rhymo?

Open Settings and select Add to Discord. Review the requested permissions before authorizing installation.

How do I select a server?

Sign in with Discord, open Server Management, and select Load servers. You must have the required management permissions on a server.

Privacy Policy

Information processed

When you sign in with Discord, Rhymo requests the Discord identity and server-listing scopes and receives profile and server information made available by those permissions.

Why it is used

This information is used to authenticate your session, display your account, and list servers where your account has management permissions.

Storage and providers

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The authentication backend and PostgreSQL session storage are hosted through Render. Discord processes the authorization request.

Cookies and sessions

Rhymo uses an HTTP-only, secure session cookie for authentication. The website and backend use different domains, so browser privacy settings may affect whether the cookie is accepted.

Retention and choices

Server-side sessions are configured to expire after seven days. Logging out requests deletion of the active session. You can revoke Rhymo's access from your Discord authorized applications settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Rhymo operator through an official support channel published by Rhymo.
