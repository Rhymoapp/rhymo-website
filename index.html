Support

Help Center

Quick answers for the things people usually get stuck on.

How do I sign in?

Hit Continue with Discord, approve the permissions, and you’ll come back signed in. That’s it — no extra account to create.

Why can't I log in?

Most of the time it’s a mismatch on the Discord redirect URL, or the backend isn’t up. Safari can also block the login cookie — try another browser if it fails there.

How do I add Rhymo?

Open Settings and click Add to Discord, or use any Add to Discord button on the site. Glance at the permissions, then approve.

How do I select a server?

Sign in, open Server Management, load your servers, and pick one from the list. You’ll only see servers where you have manage permissions.
